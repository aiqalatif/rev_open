POLICY

Effective Date: May 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 2, 2026

Welcome to REVV. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the REVV mobile application and related services. Please read this policy carefully.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as information gathered automatically when you use REVV.

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile photo when you register.
  • Vehicle Listings: Photos, videos, descriptions, pricing, and location of vehicles you post.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, pages viewed, videos watched, and search history.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, unique device identifiers, and IP address.
  • Location Data: Approximate or precise location when you permit it, for listing discovery and search.
  • Communications: Messages sent through the in-app chat system.
  • Payment & Subscription Data: Subscription tier (Revv Pro / Revv Dealer) processed via RevenueCat. We do not store card details.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the REVV platform and TikTok-style video feed.
  • Personalize your experience and surface relevant vehicle listings.
  • Facilitate communication between buyers and sellers via in-app chat.
  • Process and manage your Revv Pro or Revv Dealer subscription.
  • Send push notifications about new listings, messages, and app updates (FCM).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, push notifications, and storage infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management for Revv Pro and Revv Dealer tiers.
  • Other Users: Public listing data and your display name are visible to other REVV users.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of REVV and its users.

Subscriptions

REVV offers two paid subscription tiers:

  • Revv Pro – Enhanced features for individual buyers and sellers.
  • Revv Dealer – Advanced tools and higher listing limits for dealerships.

Subscriptions are managed through your App Store account and processed via RevenueCat. Billing, cancellations, and refunds are governed by Apple's or Google's policies.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Some data may be retained for legal or fraud-prevention purposes after account deletion.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information from within the app.
  • Opt out of marketing notifications via app settings or device settings.
  • Revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.
  • Request a copy of your data or its deletion by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

REVV is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including Firestore security rules, encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), and Firebase Authentication to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of REVV after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
